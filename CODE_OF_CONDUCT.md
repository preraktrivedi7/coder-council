# Community code of conduct

Coder Council is built in public by people with different backgrounds, experience,
and perspectives. Participation requires respectful, constructive collaboration.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code without attacking people.
- Assume good intent while still asking for evidence.
- Welcome beginners and explain project-specific context.
- Respect privacy and never pressure anyone to share credentials or private data.
- Give credit, accept correction, and keep review feedback actionable.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and repeated
disruption are not accepted in project spaces.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these standards
and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Report conduct concerns
privately through the repository owner's contact options rather than opening a
public issue. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
